# FoodSpot Mobile — Carousel Design Guide

## Brand Palette
- **Primary Green:** #006C49 (dark, trust)
- **Accent Green:** #15803D (vibrant, CTA)
- **Secondary Teal:** #006687 (contrast)
- **Light Background:** #F8F9FF (off-white)
- **Dark Text:** #151C26 (navy)
- **White:** #FFFFFF

## Typography
- **Headings:** Outfit 600–900, #151C26 or #FFFFFF
- **Body:** Inter 400–500, #151C26 or #FFFFFF (on bg)
- **CTA:** Outfit 700, #FFFFFF on #15803D

## Layout Grid
- **IG Feed:** 1080×1350px (portrait)
- **TikTok:** 1080×1920px (portrait)
- **Padding:** 40px (margins), 24px (element spacing)

## Visual Patterns
- Hero images: Food, phone mockups, hands, restaurant energy
- Icons: Lucide (arrow-right, star, rocket, check-mark)
- Animations: Fade-in, slide, subtle pulse on CTAs
- Transitions: 0.4s easing for smooth carousel flow

1. **UGC Camera** — "Convierte cada cliente en contenido"
