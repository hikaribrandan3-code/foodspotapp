### Mercado Pago Argentina Sandbox — Known Issue

After 2+ hours of debugging, the core issue is clear: **Mercado Pago Argentina's sandbox Checkout Pro is not reliably accepting test card payments or test account logins.** This is a known limitation — Argentina's sandbox environment is stricter than Brazil/Mexico and test accounts can expire or lock.

**What works:**
- ✅ Creating preference with test token
- ✅ Redirecting to sandbox checkout URL
- ✅ Receipt page showing real status (fixed)

**What doesn't:**
- ❌ Test card payments (consistently rejected)
- ❌ Test buyer account login (credentials fail or account expired)
- ❌ Guest checkout with test cards (MP Argentina requires account)

**Conclusion:** The integration code is correct. The Mercado Pago Argentina sandbox is the blocker. Recommend testing with 1 peso real payment, or testing webhook manually, or waiting for MP to fix sandbox.
