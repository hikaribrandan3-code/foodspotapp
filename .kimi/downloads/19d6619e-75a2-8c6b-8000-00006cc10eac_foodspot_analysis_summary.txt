Q1: WHY DO RESTAURANTS CHOOSE TOAST OVER SQUARE?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
→ Toast = ONE VENDOR for everything (POS + loyalty + payroll + online ordering)
→ Built restaurant-first (coursing, KDS, table management)
→ 24/7 support + dedicated onboarding consultant
→ 2-year contracts lock customers in

→ Square = FLEXIBILITY and COST CONTROL
→ Month-to-month, no contracts
→ Free tier available
→ Quick setup (same day)

REAL DIFFERENTIATOR: Toast for operators who want hand-holding; Square for 
operators who want independence. Both are transactional - neither has emotional 
engagement.

Q2: WHAT PREVENTS TOAST/SQUARE FROM ADDING ARCADE/UGC?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
1. TRANSACTION-FOCUSED ARCHITECTURE - built for speed, not entertainment
2. CORPORATE CULTURE - games = "frivolous" to operational DNA
3. RISK AVERSION - large companies punish failure more than reward innovation
4. TECHNICAL DEBT - proprietary hardware limits game engine options
5. CUSTOMER MISMATCH - neither segment is asking for arcade features

TIME TO COPY: 18-36 months (if they even try)
LIKELIHOOD OF COPY: LOW
