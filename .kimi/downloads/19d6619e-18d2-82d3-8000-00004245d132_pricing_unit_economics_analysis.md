## 1. Pricing Psychology: $15 vs $19 vs $29 vs $49

### The Science Behind Charm Pricing

Research from Quantitative Marketing and Economics shows:
- Prices ending in "9" can increase sales by up to **24%**
- Items priced at $39 outsold identical items at $34
- The left-digit effect creates significant psychological barriers

| Price | Psychological Category | Perceived Value | Conversion Impact |
|-------|----------------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| $15/mo | "Mid-teens" | Budget option | High volume, low perceived value |
| $19/mo | "Late teens" | **Sweet spot** | Optimal conversion (+15-24%) |
| $29/mo | "Late twenties" | Professional | Signals quality, lower churn |
| $49/mo | "Late forties" | Premium | Filters for serious customers |

### Key Psychological Differences

**$15/MONTH - "BUDGET TIER"**
- Perceived as "cheap" - attracts price-sensitive customers
- Higher churn (8% monthly) - customers less committed
- Lower CAC ($150) - easier to convert
- LTV:CAC ratio of 1.0x - BARELY profitable
- Risk: Attracts customers who churn at first inconvenience

**$19/MONTH - "CHARM PRICING SWEET SPOT"**
- Left-digit effect: Still "teens" psychologically
- Charm pricing: "9" ending increases conversions 15-24%
- Moderate churn (6% monthly) - reasonable retention
- Healthy unit economics: LTV:CAC of 1.3x
- **OPTIMAL for solo operators, food trucks, small restaurants**

**$29/MONTH - "PROFESSIONAL TIER"**
- Crosses into "twenties" - signals serious product
- 58% of successful micro-SaaS launch at $29-49
- Lower churn (4% monthly) - more committed customers
- Strong unit economics: LTV:CAC of 2.0x
- Best for established small restaurants

**$49/MONTH - "PREMIUM TIER"**
- Filters out casual users, attracts serious operators
- Lowest churn (3% monthly) - highly committed
- Requires sales process - higher CAC ($450)
- Excellent unit economics: LTV:CAC of 3.0x
- Best for multi-location, high-volume operators

## 3. Churn Rate Benchmarks by Price Point

| Price | Monthly Churn | Annual Churn | Customer Lifetime |
|-------|--------------|--------------|-------------------|
| $15/mo | 8% | 63.2% | 12.5 months |
| $19/mo | 6% | 52.4% | 16.7 months |
| $29/mo | 4% | 38.7% | 25.0 months |
| $49/mo | 3% | 30.6% | 33.3 months |

**Key Insight:** Higher prices do NOT necessarily produce higher churn. Products in the $79-149/month range often have LOWER monthly churn (3-5%) than products at $9-19/month (8-12%). Why? Customers who pay more treat the tool more seriously, integrate it more deeply, and are less likely to cancel impulsively.

## 7. Freemium vs Free Trial Recommendation

### Conversion Benchmarks

| Model | Signup Rate | Free→Paid | Time to Convert |
|-------|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Freemium | 13-16% | 2-5% | 90-180 days |
| Free Trial (opt-in) | 8-12% | 18-25% | 12-18 days |
| Free Trial (CC req) | 3-5% | 48-60% | 7-14 days |

### ✅ RECOMMENDATION: 14-Day Free Trial (Opt-in, No CC Required)

**Why this model for restaurant tech:**
- 18-25% conversion rate (vs 2-5% for freemium)
- Faster monetization (12-18 days vs 90-180 days)
- Lower support burden (fewer free users)
- Creates urgency - trial expires
- Better for B2B/restaurant decisions

**Optimal Trial Structure:**
- **Trial Length:** 14 days (most common, 62% of SaaS)
- **Credit Card:** Not required (opt-in)
- **Limitations:** Full features, 50 order limit, 1 location
- **Post-Trial:** Read-only mode, 30-day grace period
