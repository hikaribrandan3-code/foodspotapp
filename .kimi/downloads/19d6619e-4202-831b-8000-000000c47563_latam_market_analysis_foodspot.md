## 7. REGULATORY & TAX COMPLICATIONS

### SaaS TAXATION BY COUNTRY

| Country | VAT Rate | Digital Services Tax | Withholding | Complexity |
|---------|----------|---------------------|-------------|------------|
| **Argentina** | 21% | Yes (2018) | 31.5% WHT | ⭐⭐⭐ High |
| **Brazil** | 17-20% | CBS/IBS reform 2026 | Various | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High |
| **Mexico** | 16% | Yes (2020) | 25% royalties | ⭐⭐⭐ High |
| **Colombia** | 19% | Yes (2018) | 20% WHT | ⭐⭐⭐ High |
| **Chile** | 19% | Yes (2020) | 15-35% WHT | ⭐⭐⭐ High |
| **Peru** | 18% | Yes (2024) | Variable | ⭐⭐⭐ High |

### KEY REGULATORY INSIGHTS

**Argentina:**
- SaaS subject to 21% VAT (import of digital services)
- Withholding tax on foreign services: 31.5% effective
- **Mitigation:** Local entity or partner with Argentine company

**Brazil:**
- Complex multi-level tax system (ISS, PIS, COFINS, ICMS)
- 2026 CBS/IBS reform may simplify but increase rates (~28%)
- **Mitigation:** Stripe Atlas US entity + local payment processing

**Mexico:**
- 16% VAT on digital services
- 25% withholding on "royalties" (software licenses)
- **Mitigation:** Structure as service, not license; use tax treaties

**Recommended Structure:**
1. US LLC (Delaware/Wyoming) via Stripe Atlas
2. Process payments through Stripe US
3. Local payment gateways for cash/local methods
4. Professional tax advisor in each country
