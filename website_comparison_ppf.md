## 2. HEADLINE & COPY ANALYSIS

### Executive ENM — SEO-First, Feature-Driven
```
H1: "Premium PPF Installation for Ultimate Paint Protection"
H2: "Ultimate Paint Shield Solution"
Body: "Protect your vehicle's paint with high-performance STEK 
       Paint Protection Film. Our self-healing, stain-resistant, 
       and ultra-durable PPF shields against scratches..."
```
**Analysis:**
- ✅ Keyword-rich ("PPF Installation", "Paint Protection Film")
- ✅ Technical specs mentioned (STEK brand, self-healing)
- ✅ Multiple H1/H2 tags for SEO
- ❌ Generic, could be any shop in any city
- ❌ No emotional hook

### Area 51 Detailing — Punchy, Problem-Focused
```
Headline: "Just Took Delivery?"
Subhead: "YOUR NEW CAR IS PERFECT. FOR NOW."
Body: "One rock chip at highway speed and you're looking at a 
       $800 body shop visit — if they can even match the paint."
```
**Analysis:**
- ✅ Strong emotional hook (fear of damage)
- ✅ Specific pain point ($800 repair cost)
- ✅ Conversational tone
- ❌ Missing SEO keywords
- ❌ No mention of film brands (XPEL, STEK, 3M)

**WINNER: Tie** — Executive wins SEO, Area 51 wins emotional impact
